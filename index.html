FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is the domain really free?

Yes, for the first year. From the second year, renewal of the domain and hosting costs ₱1,500 to ₱2,500 per year depending on your package. We'll remind you before it's due.

How do I pay?

50% downpayment to start and 50% before the website goes live. We accept GCash.

How long does it take?

Starter takes 5–7 days, Business 7–10 days and Premium 14–21 days. The clock starts once we have your downpayment and your content.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, photos, menu or product list with prices, and contact details. No logo yet? We can design one. No photos? We can help you choose good stock photos to start.

Can I update the website myself?

With Premium, yes: the admin dashboard lets you edit products, prices and stock. For Starter and Business, our ₱499/month maintenance plan covers updates, or we can do one-time changes for a small fee.

Can customers pay online?

Yes. Business includes ordering with your GCash QR code. Premium adds a full cart and checkout.

Do you only work with businesses near you?

No. We work online with businesses anywhere in the Philippines, through Messenger and video calls.
