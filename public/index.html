FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is the domain really free?

Yes, for the first year. From the second year, renewal of the domain and hosting costs ₱1,500 to ₱2,500 per year depending on your package. We'll remind you before it's due.

How do I pay?

50% downpayment to start and 50% before the website goes live. We accept GCash.

How long does it take?

Starter takes 5–7 days, Business 7–10 days and Premium 14–21 days. The clock starts once we have your downpayment and your content. Custom systems get their own timeline in the written scope.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, photos, menu or product list with prices, and contact details. No logo yet? We can design one. No photos? We can help you choose good stock photos to start.

Can I update the website myself?

With Premium, yes: the admin dashboard lets you edit products, prices and stock. For Starter and Business, our ₱499/month maintenance plan covers updates, or we can do one-time changes for a small fee.

Can customers pay online?

Yes. Business includes ordering with your GCash QR code. Premium adds a full cart and checkout. A verified payment gateway can be added as a custom option.

Can you build a POS, inventory or payroll system without a website?

Yes. Business systems can be built on their own or connected to your website. Advanced modules like CRM, attendance, payroll and workflow automation are quoted separately after a free consultation, because every business works differently.

Are the sample sites real businesses?

No. Brew & Co., Kusina ni Lola, Lakas Fitness, Ganda Studio, Handaan Catering, Linaw Accounting and Tahanan Realty are sample sites we built to show what's possible. Kape Pinagpala, SleepScienzzz and Pabango de Manila are real client websites.

Is my business data safe?

Every site runs on HTTPS. Business systems use individual staff logins, role-based permissions checked on the server, and we never share your data or customer information.

Do you only work with businesses near you?

No. We work online with businesses anywhere in the Philippines, through Messenger and video calls.
