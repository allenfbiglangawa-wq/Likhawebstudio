It started with Lola Remy's kitchen.

For over 30 years, Lola Remy cooked for every birthday, fiesta and Sunday lunch on our street in Marikina. Neighbors kept asking if they could order, so in 2020 her grandchildren turned the family kitchen into Kusina ni Lola.

We still use her recipes, her clay palayok for the kare-kare, and fresh ingredients from Marikina public market every morning. No shortcuts, no MSG.

FAQ

What time is the order cut-off?

10 AM for lunch delivery (11 AM – 1 PM) and 3 PM for dinner (5 PM – 7 PM). Party trays need at least 2 days' notice.

Can I pre-order for a future date?

Yes, you can schedule up to 6 days ahead. Just pick the date and time slot at checkout.

How do I pay?

GCash (scan our QR and upload the receipt) or cash on delivery. For party packages, we ask for a 50% downpayment.

Is the food spicy?

Only dishes marked “Spicy”. Bicol Express can be made mild, just add a note.

Do you cater events?

Yes! We cater for 30 to 200 guests with buffet setup and servers. Message us on Facebook for a quote.
