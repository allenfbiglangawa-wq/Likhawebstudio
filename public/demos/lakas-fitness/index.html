FAQ

Good to know

Can I try the gym before signing up?

Yes. Buy a Day Pass at the front desk or online. If you sign up for a Monthly plan the same week, many gyms credit it to your membership.

I'm a beginner. Where do I start?

Book a Beginner Fitness class or a personal training session. A coach will show you how to use the equipment safely.

Do I need to book group classes?

Yes, slots are limited so everyone gets enough space. Book here on the website or in the member portal.

Is there a locker and shower?

Yes. Bring your own lock, or rent one at the front desk.

How is my personal data handled?

On a live site, registrations are stored in the gym's private database, only staff can see them, and they're used only to manage your membership, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. This demo stores nothing.
