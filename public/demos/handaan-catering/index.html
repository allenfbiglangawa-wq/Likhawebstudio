      FAQ

      Good to know

      What is the minimum number of guests?

      50 guests for birthdays, christenings and corporate events, 80 for debuts, and 100 for weddings. For smaller groups, ask about our party trays. (Sample policy for this demo.)

      How far ahead should we book?

      Weddings and debuts usually book 3 to 6 months ahead, especially for December weekends. The online planner accepts dates at least 14 days away; for anything sooner, send a rush request.

      Can we have a food tasting first?

      Yes. Once you have an estimate, we schedule a tasting of your shortlisted dishes. The tasting fee is deducted from your package when you book.

      Do you serve outside Metro Manila?

      Yes, within Rizal, Cavite, Laguna and Bulacan, and further by request. A transport fee applies outside Metro Manila and is shown in your estimate.

      Can you handle dietary needs?

      Turn on "No pork" in the menu builder for a pork-free buffet. Tell us about allergies in the inquiry notes and the kitchen will label and separate those dishes.

      What happens if our headcount changes?

      Final headcount is due 7 days before the event. Adding guests is usually fine; reducing below the package minimum is billed at the minimum.
