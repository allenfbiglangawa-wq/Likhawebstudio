    How buying works

    From viewing to keys, in four steps

    A typical path for buyers in the Philippines. Exact requirements depend on the seller, the developer and your lender.

    1. View and shortlist

      Save the homes you like, then book on-site or video viewings for your top picks.

    2. Reserve

      Pay the reservation fee to hold the unit while you prepare your documents.

    3. Get financing

      Apply for a bank or Pag-IBIG loan, or arrange cash or in-house terms. Your agent tracks the papers.

    4. Sign and move in

      Sign the contract, settle the down payment and fees, and get your keys at turnover.

    Can I view a property on a weekend?

    Yes. Pick any open slot in the scheduler, weekends included. If no time works, mention it in the notes and the agent will suggest one.

    Do you have rent-to-own or in-house financing?

    It depends on the property. Listings that allow it say so in their features. You can also choose “In-house financing” when you book so the agent can check for you.

    Can I list my own property with you?

    On a live site, owners can send their property details and photos through a listing form, and the brokerage reviews them before they go up.

    Are the prices final?

    On a live site, prices are set by the seller or developer and can change. In this demo, every price is a sample.
