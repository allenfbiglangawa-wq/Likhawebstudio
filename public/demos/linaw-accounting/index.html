        FAQ

        Common questions

        I haven't filed in months. Can you still help?

        Yes. We start with a review of your BIR records, list what's missing, and give you a catch-up plan with the expected cost before we file anything.

        Do I need to visit your office?

        No. Most clients work with us fully online: video calls, document uploads and e-signatures. You're welcome to visit if you prefer meeting in person.

        What do I send you each month?

        Usually your sales invoices or receipts, expense receipts, and payroll records if you have employees. We send a simple checklist after onboarding.

        Are government fees and penalties included?

        No. Our fee covers our professional services. Taxes, government filing fees and any penalties are paid directly to the agency, and we always tell you the amount in advance.

        Can I cancel the monthly retainer?

        Yes, with 30 days' notice (sample policy). We hand over your books and filing records so your next accountant can continue smoothly.
