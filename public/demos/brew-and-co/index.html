Our story

A small café with a big love for Filipino coffee.

Brew & Co. started in 2019 as a weekend coffee cart outside UP Diliman. Today we are a 30-seat café on Maginhawa, but we still do things the slow way: we buy straight from farmers in Atok and Lipa, roast in small batches, and dial in every shot by hand.

Whether you need a quick iced latte before class or a quiet table to work for the afternoon, come say hi. Free Wi-Fi and plenty of outlets.
